# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard – Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

The **HR Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze employee demographics, workforce distribution, employee attrition, salary levels, job roles, job satisfaction, gender, age groups, and experience.

The primary objective of this project is to help HR teams identify **attrition patterns and workforce trends** and use data-driven insights to improve employee retention and workforce planning.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The key objectives of this project are:

* Analyze overall employee attrition.
* Calculate and monitor the employee attrition rate.
* Identify departments with the highest attrition.
* Analyze attrition across different salary slabs.
* Compare attrition by gender.
* Analyze employee distribution across age groups.
* Identify job roles with higher attrition.
* Understand the relationship between job satisfaction and attrition.
* Analyze attrition based on employee experience.
* Provide an interactive dashboard for HR decision-making.

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 1. Overall Attrition

The organization has an attrition count of **231 employees**, representing an overall attrition rate of approximately **16.3%**.

This indicates that employee retention is an important area for HR management.

### 2. Department-wise Attrition

Administration records the highest attrition with approximately **84 employees**, representing around **36%** of total attrition.

Sales follows with **52 employees**, while Operations accounts for approximately **43 employees**.

These departments can be prioritized for deeper retention analysis.

### 3. Salary-wise Attrition

The **6–10 LPA salary slab** has the largest employee population in the dashboard.

The dashboard can help HR investigate whether compensation levels are associated with employee turnover and identify salary groups requiring further analysis.

### 4. Gender-wise Attrition

Male employees account for approximately **146 attritions (63.2%)**, while female employees account for approximately **85 attritions (36.8%)**.

This provides HR with a clear view of gender-wise attrition patterns.

### 5. Age Group Analysis

The **26–35 age group** represents the largest employee population.

This indicates that a significant portion of the workforce is in the early-to-mid career stage, making career growth and employee engagement important areas for retention.

### 6. Job Role Analysis

Among the displayed job roles:

* Laboratory Technician – **59 attritions**
* Sales Executive – **56 attritions**
* Research Scientist – **45 attritions**
* Sales Representative – **34 attritions**

These roles can be investigated further to understand the reasons behind higher employee turnover.

### 7. Experience-wise Attrition

The attrition trend is concentrated more heavily among employees with relatively lower experience.

As experience increases, the attrition trend decreases considerably.

This suggests that early-career employees may require additional attention through career development, mentoring, training and engagement initiatives.

### 8. Job Satisfaction

The dashboard provides attrition distribution across job satisfaction levels from **1 to 4**.

This allows HR teams to investigate whether lower satisfaction levels are associated with increased employee turnover.

The project involved:

1. Importing the HR dataset into Power BI.
2. Cleaning and transforming the data using Power Query.
3. Creating calculated measures using DAX.
4. Creating employee and attrition KPIs.
5. Building department, salary, gender, age and experience analyses.
6. Creating interactive visualizations.
7. Adding slicers for dynamic filtering.
8. Designing an interactive HR dashboard.
9. Extracting actionable business insights from the analysis.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the dashboard analysis, HR teams can consider:

* Investigating the reasons for high attrition in Administration and Sales.
* Reviewing compensation and benefits across salary groups.
* Creating stronger career-development opportunities for early-career employees.
* Conducting employee engagement and satisfaction surveys.
* Investigating workload and working conditions in high-attrition job roles.
* Developing targeted employee-retention programs.
* Monitoring attrition regularly through HR dashboards.
